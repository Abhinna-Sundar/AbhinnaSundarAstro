Looking for Grad Schools and PhD positions or maybe summer internships in Astronomy/Astrophysics? This post could be useful for the same purpose, for various countries and continents!

The season has come for Grad Schools/PhD applications. I have listed a few sources on how to find these positions, contacts & apply. Firstly, check [this article made by current and former PhD students](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1mHZFVskJ8uEF5xdMnsq1LRMDaQtUVggPLWg5uUNSxD4/edit#heading=h.rdztm91wgqk).

## A few important websites and job directories

- **AAS Job Register**: covers most PhD openings across Europe, the UK & Australia. <https://jobregister.aas.org>
- **EAS Job directory**: by the European Astronomical Society, covers most of the European Union (EU). <https://eas.unige.ch/jobs.jsp>
- **RASJOBS**: provides email alerts for new job (PhD/others) postings. <https://ras.ac.uk/membership/RAS-job-list>
- **German Astronomical Society (AG)**: subscribe to their job announcement mailing list. <https://astronomische-gesellschaft.de/en/activities/jobregister>
- **INSPIRE HEP**: search for 'astrophysics/astronomy' in the job filter. <https://inspirehep.net>
- **ETH Zurich job listings**: only ETH Zurich positions. <https://jobs.ethz.ch/jobmail/?lang=en>

## More sources and connections

- Search individual universities' webpages. Check the department, contact the supervisor you are interested in, and email them (please attach a CV and SoP). *Do not use Dear Sir/Ma'am/Prof. Use Dear Prof. [Surname]. You are not writing a generic email but to a specific person.*
- Look for PhD positions on Twitter! People (actually) post the vacant positions in their department there. A simple search like 'Astrophysics PhD positions' gives you the latest job postings.
- Join Facebook groups. Many FB groups share the latest PhD positions, e.g. Early Career Astronomers, Python Hacks for Astronomy, etc.

Apart from this, here is some important information for applying for PhD positions in the EU, UK, US, Australia/NZ and India (I was offered PhDs from various institutions in India too).

## PhD positions in India

- NET JRF (usually twice a year)
- GATE (once a year, around February)
- JEST (once a year, around March)
- IIT JAM for Int-PhD (must have a BSc degree)
- TIFR GS exam
- INAT exam, for IUCAA
- INSPIRE Fellowship
- Institute/University fellowships
- Check Astronomical Society of India (ASI) jobs: <https://www.astron-soc.in/jobs>

## PhD positions in the EU

- Usually there is no English proficiency requirement.
- Applications are open roughly the whole year.
- DAAD Fellowship: contact the people you are interested in and apply to them.
- Max Planck Institutes: [check this thread](https://twitter.com/Cosmoparticle/status/1688571951860621312).

## PhD positions in Australia

- English proficiency required (TOEFL/IELTS).
- No application fees.
- Good grades are required (usually >8.5/10 in the Indian format).
- Check the Go8 (Group of Eight research-intensive universities).

## PhD positions in the USA

- English proficiency; some require the GRE (this has been changing). [Check this sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/19UhYToXOPZkZ3CM469ru3Uwk4584CmzZyAVVwQJJcyc/edit#gid=0).
- Application fees (except at a few universities); see the sheet above. Ask for waivers (by email, or attend intro sessions where they share a code at the end).
- One year of mandatory coursework.
- Top universities also require exceptional grades!

## PhD positions in the UK

- English proficiency required.
- Usually no application fees.
- Scholarships are available, but even after you clear the interview, *not* every advertised position/project will receive funding!
- Joint PhD programmes between IITs and UK universities.

## Summer internships

- Check the AAS: <https://aas.org/careers/internships-summer-jobs>
- Apply for DAAD and try for summer/winter internships in the EU.
- For Australia: <https://astronomy.org.au/education/summer-vacation-schorlarships/>
- [Astrobites](https://twitter.com/astrobites) and [Astrobetter](https://twitter.com/astrobetter).
- Summer internships in India (IAS, NCRA-TIFR, KSP…).
- Check Twitter and Facebook posts regularly.
